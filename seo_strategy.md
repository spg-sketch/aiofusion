# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public AIO Fusion marketing, insights, legal, and pre-rendered resource pages.
- Public promotional microsite pages where source is part of this project. The formerly scanned `artifacts/aio-fusion-promo` artifact is absent from the current source tree as of task 454.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated platform, admin, billing, support, and internal tool routes.
- API endpoints and development-only mockup previews.
- Internal build-plan and roadmap documents, rather than public marketing content.

## Target audience
- PR agencies, in-house communications teams, and AI/search visibility practitioners.

## Primary keywords
- AI search visibility, GEO platform, AI authority, PR and marketing teams, earned media.

## Crawler assumptions
- Googlebot may render JavaScript, but social bots and AI crawlers are evaluated against pre-rendered/static HTML.
- The AIO Fusion marketing routes are expected to be statically pre-rendered by the build.
- Insights are CMS-backed with a checked-in fallback snapshot; CMS article coverage and metadata must remain consistent in static HTML and client navigation.
- Source-only scan results do not verify deployed routing, current database content or live crawler access.
