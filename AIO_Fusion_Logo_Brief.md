# AIO Fusion — Logo Design Brief

## 1. About the product

**AIO Fusion** is a Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) platform built by **Simpatico PR**, a B2B PR and communications agency. The platform helps agencies and B2B businesses become the source that AI engines — ChatGPT and Claude — cite and recommend.

It is a managed-service tool: Simpatico PR staff operate the platform on behalf of clients, delivering GEO as part of a retained PR engagement. The platform diagnoses how AI sees a brand, optimises content for AI retrieval, and plans authority-building activity.

**Key concept:** "Fusion" reflects the merging of earned media, structured data, and AI-readable content into a single authority signal. "AIO" stands for AI Optimisation.

---

## 2. Brand positioning

- **Category:** B2B SaaS / MarTech / PR technology
- **Audience:** PR agencies, B2B marketing teams, communications directors
- **Tone:** Authoritative, modern, confident — but not aggressive. Premium without being cold. Approachable expertise.
- **Competitive space:** This is a new category (GEO). The logo should feel pioneering and forward-looking, not like a generic analytics dashboard.

---

## 3. Parent brand relationship

AIO Fusion is a product of **Simpatico PR**. The logo should be able to stand alone as a product mark, but should not clash with Simpatico's existing brand identity. It may occasionally appear alongside a "by Simpatico PR" lockup.

---

## 4. Colour palette

The platform uses the following established palette:

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Primary accent** | `#E8472A` | Coral/red-orange — CTAs, highlights, energy |
| **Teal** | `#2B8C8C` | Secondary accent — trust, data, AI signals |
| **Navy** | `#1A1A2E` | Dark background — the dominant canvas colour |
| **Slate** | `#4A6FA5` | Tertiary — structure, depth |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark backgrounds |

The primary logo should work in:
- Full colour on dark navy (`#1A1A2E`) background
- Full colour on white background
- Single colour (white) on dark backgrounds
- Single colour (navy or black) on light backgrounds

The coral accent `#E8472A` is the signature brand colour and should feature prominently in the logomark.

---

## 5. Typography context

The platform uses:
- **DM Serif Display** for all headings (elegant, editorial serif)
- **Inter** for body text (clean, modern sans-serif)

The wordmark "AIO Fusion" currently uses DM Serif Display. The designer is free to explore custom lettering, but the final wordmark should feel consistent with this serif + sans pairing — sophisticated but not decorative.

---

## 6. Logomark direction

The current placeholder is a simple icon (sparkles/settings). The final logomark should communicate one or more of these ideas:

- **Fusion / convergence** — multiple signals merging into one authoritative source
- **AI / intelligence** — without resorting to generic robot or brain clichés
- **Signal / visibility** — being seen, being cited, standing out in AI responses
- **Authority / trust** — the confidence of being the definitive source

**Visual approaches to explore:**
- Abstract mark suggesting convergence or signal amplification
- Lettermark using "AIO" with a distinctive stylistic treatment
- A combination mark (symbol + wordmark) that can scale from favicon (16px) to hero usage

**Avoid:**
- Generic AI imagery (robot heads, neural networks, circuit boards)
- Overly complex marks that won't work at small sizes
- Anything that looks like a search engine or traditional SEO tool
- Gradients that can't be reproduced in single-colour applications

---

## 7. Deliverables

1. **Primary logo** — Combination mark (symbol + "AIO Fusion" wordmark)
2. **Logomark only** — The symbol alone, for favicons, app icons, avatars
3. **Wordmark only** — "AIO Fusion" text treatment without symbol
4. **Lockup variant** — "AIO Fusion by Simpatico PR" for co-branded use
5. **Colour variants** — Full colour, white (reversed), dark (navy/black)
6. **File formats** — SVG, PNG (transparent), and favicon-ready sizes

---

## 8. Usage context

The logo will appear in:
- Web application nav bar (dark navy background, ~32-40px height)
- Landing page header
- PDF reports and guides
- Browser favicon / tab icon
- Email signatures and social media profiles
- Presentation decks alongside the Simpatico PR brand

---

## 9. Reference / mood

- The platform UI is dark, premium, editorial — think Bloomberg Terminal meets a modern SaaS dashboard
- Inspiration: Stripe's wordmark clarity, Linear's geometric precision, Vercel's bold simplicity
- The "For Agents" section of the site (inspired by Bluhalo) reinforces the technical credibility — the logo should feel at home next to API documentation and structured data

---

The existing icon in the nav is a rounded coral square with a settings/sparkles icon inside it. This is a placeholder only and should be replaced by the final logomark.
