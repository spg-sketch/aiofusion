## 3. Safeguards and rights handling

- Data minimisation and field-level access; no public database lookup.
- Account-free requests receive a neutral confirmation that does not reveal
  whether a person or email exists.
- Verify identity proportionately. Email ownership alone is not proof of
  entitlement; uncertain matches require human review. Access disclosure,
  canonical correction, suppression or removal require recorded verification
  and explicit authorised reviewer approval.
- A confirmed upheld objection/removal prevents reintroduction across the
  approved scope, including imports, manual entry, discovery, recommendations,
  exports and enrichment. A private-workspace decision does not silently become
  a platform-wide suppression.
- Preserve audit chronology, but erase or redact personal payloads in live and
  denormalised copies where the approved outcome requires it. Record any lawful
  hold rather than claiming erasure.
- **Legal holds:** [hold authority, scope, data, start/review/end dates,
  approving lawyer]. **No hold is assumed; exception rules and legal approval
  are pending.**
- **Backup/restore:** [backup retention, restore test, suppression replay,
  access controls and incident procedure]. A restore must not silently
  reintroduce suppressed data. **Infrastructure/legal approval pending.**
- **Security incident route:** [link to incident procedure and notification
