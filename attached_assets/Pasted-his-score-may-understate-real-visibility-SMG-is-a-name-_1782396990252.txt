Method & caveats

How the audit works, what the numbers mean, and what to watch out for

Blind probes were run across ChatGPT and Claude using 8 non-branded category queries a prospect, journalist or researcher might ask. SMG was never named in the prompts.
Presence is the share of probes in which SMG appeared. Share of voice weighs those mentions against the rival brands the engines named.
AI responses are not deterministic, so individual results vary. The trend across repeated cycles is the meaningful signal, not a single run.
Some ICP-narrowed probes are phrased to surface specialist or independent providers rather than large household names — a global agency or enterprise vendor is not expected to appear in those specific questions.
Scorecard dimensions marked N/M (not measurable) could not be scored reliably because the brand appeared in too few probes. They are not failures — they indicate where more visibility work is needed first.
