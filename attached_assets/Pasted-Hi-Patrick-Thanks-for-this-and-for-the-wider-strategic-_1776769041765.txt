Before I dig into the components, the customer journey , in the order a real user will move through is listed at the end of this email.
So... whilst eventually A new in-house marketing manager or agency account lead lands on the marketing site, sees the new 'Business visibility for the AI Age' positioning and the For In-house or For Agencies page that speaks to their model, and clicks through to Platform Login,
Inside the platform they hit the dashboard with a 'Create client programme' empty state, which drops them into Client Intake. 
Intake is split into Tech (site, tracking, integrations) and Content (brand, messaging, spokespeople, target media), with spokespeople captured as a structured list (name, title, expertise) and media as categorised URL lists (Priority, National, Specialist A to D). Once intake is saved, they run the AIO Diagnostic and Earned Visibility checks under AIO Audit to see where the brand currently sits in AI answers, then move through Website AI Optimisation (SEO Assessment and GEO Content Optimisation) to fix the foundations. From there they open the Authority Planner, build out the forward calendar week by week, and watch the Visibility plus Authority scores update live as they choose content type, channels, spokesperson and status. When a project is ready to write, they jump into the Content Optimiser, where the spokesperson and target media pickers are already populated from intake, draft the piece in the rich editor, run Optimise, and either Approve and archive, Share for review, Push to Planner with the projected score, or Download. Approved drafts flow into Release Gateway for manual download, LinkedIn share or one of the wire connectors, and every piece is stored and tagged in Archive for search and reuse. Finally the team opens Measure and Report to see the outcome, with Released Content showing audience reach, pieces by type and media, visibility per piece, coverage per key message, social impact, ideas-to-outcomes ratio, and breakdown by spokesperson. The whole loop, intake to measurement, is now walkable in a single sitting.

I took a considered view on your scoring system note. Rather than hardcode a specific formula, I built the engine so it is fully tunable from the UI via a 'Scoring settings' button in the Planner header. You can edit the visibility and authority weights for each content type (each out of 10), adjust the channel multiplier base, step and cap that control how much extra visibility each added channel contributes, set the status multipliers (Planned, Drafting, Review, Approved) that discount work-in-progress against delivery confidence, and add or remove content types and channels without a code change. Settings save per browser and projections recalculate live. The benefit is that you and the team can calibrate the model with prospects in the room rather than waiting on a developer cycle every time the weighting needs to move.
