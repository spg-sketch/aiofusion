Method & caveats

How the audit works, what the numbers mean, and what to watch out for

Blind probes were run across ChatGPT and Claude using 8 non-branded category queries a prospect, journalist or researcher might ask. SMG was never named in the prompts.
Presence is the share of probes in which SMG appeared. Share of voice weighs those mentions against the rival brands the engines named.
AI responses are not deterministic, so individual results vary. The trend across repeated cycles is the meaningful signal, not a single run.
Some ICP-narrowed probes are phrased to surface specialist or independent providers rather than large household names — a global agency or enterprise vendor is not expected to appear in those specific questions.
Scorecard dimensions marked N/M (not measurable) could not be scored reliably because the brand appeared in too few probes. They are not failures — they indicate where more visibility work is needed first.

How to interpret these results

A practical guide to making sense of your score and acting on it

1
This is the real test. We're asking AI models the same questions a prospect, journalist or researcher would ask. If your brand doesn't appear, that's the gap GEO work needs to close.

2

3
Competitors reveal what's working. The companies AI does mention are winning the visibility race in your sector - analyse what they're doing differently with content structure, schema markup and authority signals.

4
Use this as a baseline. Run it now, do your GEO work (diagnostic, content optimisation, schema, authority planning), then run it again. A score moving from 20% to 50% is a measurable result you can report to the client.

5
Set the right business type. Use the Business type selector to match the probe vocabulary to the client — agencies and providers for services, platforms and vendors for products, brands for consumer. This ensures the competitor set AI surfaces is actually comparable to the client.
