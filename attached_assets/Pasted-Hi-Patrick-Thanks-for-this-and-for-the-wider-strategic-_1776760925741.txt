I took a considered view on your scoring system note. Rather than hardcode a specific formula, I built the engine so it is fully tunable from the UI via a 'Scoring settings' button in the Planner header. You can edit the visibility and authority weights for each content type (each out of 10), adjust the channel multiplier base, step and cap that control how much extra visibility each added channel contributes, set the status multipliers (Planned, Drafting, Review, Approved) that discount work-in-progress, and add or remove content types and channels. Changes persist per browser and preview live. The current defaults broadly mirror the weighting logic in your example, so we can sit down and calibrate against your planner together rather than me second-guessing the numbers. There is also safety behaviour built in: if you remove a channel or content type, existing projects are normalised rather than quietly inflating scores against data that no longer exists.

Release Gateway is unlocked with manual download, LinkedIn share and seven wire connector buttons stubbed in. Archive is unlocked with tagged storage of drafts and finals plus search and filter by project type, message, title and spokesperson. Measure & Report has been restructured into four tabs: Action Plan, Executive Summary, Detailed Audit, and Released Content. The last of those carries the outcome metrics that matter for the reasons-to-buy story: audience reach, pieces released, visibility per piece, ideas to outcomes, coverage per key message, volume by content type, volume by media tier, social impact and volume by spokesperson.
