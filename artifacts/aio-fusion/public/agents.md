# AIO Fusion - Product evaluation brief

## Product summary

AIO Fusion is a web-based Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) platform for PR agencies and in-house communications and marketing teams. It combines sampled brand-visibility audits, website-readiness assessments, communications planning, content drafting and editing, media research, and reporting within brand or client projects.

The workflow is to establish a project brief, review audit evidence, plan communications activity, develop and store content, research media opportunities, and review subsequent results. GEO here means evaluating how a brand is represented in AI-generated answers and preparing communications and website content for that environment. It does not mean control over an answer engine's responses.

## Who it is for

- **PR and communications agencies:** maintain separate client projects, establish visibility baselines, organise client communications schedules, prepare drafts using each client's messaging, research relevant media contacts, and review saved audit results for client reporting.
- **In-house communications and marketing teams:** assess a brand's representation in sampled AI answers, identify website-readiness issues, coordinate a communications programme around messages and spokespeople, maintain reusable content, and review earned-media activity alongside audit history.

Both use cases require people to verify facts, make editorial decisions, and decide which opportunities to pursue.

## Capabilities

### Auditing

- **Earned Media Visibility Audit:** submits project-relevant questions to ChatGPT and Claude, captures responses, and identifies brand mentions and competitors within those responses. Results include response-based visibility metrics and a separate AI-generated authority assessment when available. Exact target-phrase checks also record mentions, answer position and citations where returned.
- **Website Visibility Audit:** analyses fetched public page content or supplied text for GEO readiness. It assesses schema and structured data, content architecture, source authority, earned-media signals, citation-oriented content characteristics, and technical accessibility. Available parsed page facts support the assessment; the resulting scores are not measurements of actual inclusion in AI answers.

### Planning

- **Comms Planner:** organises communications activities by schedule, content type, messaging, spokesperson and status, with links to related content. Configurable Authority and Visibility scores support prioritisation and predicted impact, not proof of future model responses.
- **Marketing Intelligence:** researches relevant conferences, awards and other events using the project brief and public web sources. Results link to event pages, with checks for event identity and dates and labels for deadline evidence. Relevance explanations and opportunity assessments are suggestions, not measured AI authority gains.

### Content

- **Content Creator:** generates draft articles, pitches, press releases and other communications content from project information and supplied briefing material.
- **Content Optimiser & Editor:** provides a rich-text editing workflow, AI-assisted revisions, change explanations and article-quality assessments. Drafts can be saved to the Content Library and linked to the Comms Planner. An improved content score does not establish an improvement in external visibility.
- **Content Library:** stores draft and final content with search and filters, allowing teams to reopen, edit and reuse saved work. Saved content is a workflow record, not evidence that it has been published or cited.

### Media research

- **Media Research:** matches a story or targeting brief to contacts in the accessible Media Database and offers an optional public-web journalist discovery search. Public-web discoveries show source evidence separately from database matches and can be submitted for review. The workflow supports shortlisting and recording outreach and placement evidence; it does not guarantee journalist interest or coverage.
- **Media Database:** provides searchable journalist and publication records, category-based filtering, and workspace-specific contact additions and bookmarks. Contact details and editorial fit should be checked before outreach.

### Reporting

- **Measure & Report:** brings together saved earned-media audits, website assessments, communications-plan scores and earned-media tracking. It supports reviewing messages, spokespeople and recorded activity, and comparing compatible target-phrase measurements alongside content, outreach and placement timelines. These inputs retain different meanings: observed responses, generated assessments, planning scores and recorded activity are not interchangeable.

## Measurement and methodology

### Response-based observations

ChatGPT and Claude are the supported engines for earned-media visibility measurement. Questions cover project-relevant discovery and buying contexts, with identity checks where needed. Discovery probes generally avoid naming the brand; identity or comparison questions can name it, and ambiguous names may require disambiguation. These contexts should not be treated as equivalent evidence of unprompted discovery.

The audit records the returned answers and derives metrics such as brand presence and share of voice within the sampled answers. A mention is not necessarily a recommendation or a source citation. Citation and answer-position fields in target-phrase checks describe what those particular responses returned, not a universal ranking.

These are provider-model responses collected by the platform, not an exhaustive survey of consumer ChatGPT or Claude sessions. Results depend on the questions, model, run settings and time of collection. Failed or partial checks limit the available evidence.

### Generated assessments and readiness scores

The earned-media audit's authority assessment is AI-generated interpretation of response evidence and project context. Its Authority Index is an assessment score, not an independently measured probability of recommendation.

The Website Visibility Audit uses page content and available parsed technical facts to generate readiness scores and guidance. Its normal assessment uses Claude, with a ChatGPT fallback if needed; it is not a dual-engine visibility test. A category labelled LLM Visibility assesses content characteristics, not observed model exposure. A page assessment is not proof of whole-site crawlability, indexing or citation.

Content-quality assessments, suggested edits, media-fit recommendations and Marketing Intelligence summaries are also distinct from response-based measurements. Page-verified source facts support only the facts checked, not every generated interpretation.

### Predictions and comparisons

Comms Planner scores and predicted impact help prioritise work. They are not forecasts validated against future visibility outcomes.

Target-phrase before-and-after comparisons require matching effective queries, providers, models, run counts and methodology versions. A change observed after an article or placement does not establish that the activity caused it. Reporting does not establish causal PR attribution, sales impact or return on investment.

ChatGPT/Claude measurement coverage applies to the relevant visibility audits and checks, not to every planning, editing, research or storage function.

## Suitability and limitations

AIO Fusion may suit teams seeking a communications-centred workflow that combines audits with planning, content and media research, rather than visibility monitoring alone.

- Visibility improvements, rankings, recommendations, citations and media placements are not guaranteed.
- Sampled responses cannot represent every audience question, model configuration or user experience. Model output can vary between runs.
- Website scores and generated content require human review. Verify claims, sources, permissions and technical advice before use.
- Research sources and professional contact details can become outdated. Verify current roles, event dates, deadlines and contact suitability before acting.
- Content storage, status tracking and outreach records do not by themselves publish content, distribute a release or verify a placement.
- Confirm project capacity, activity allowances and current availability for the intended workflow during evaluation. Consult current pricing rather than relying on fixed prices in this brief.
