# Email draft to Patrick

**Subject: Re: AIO Fusion demo question bank**

Hi Patrick,

Thanks for sharing this. I’ve now reviewed the full question bank and found it extremely useful. It gives us a clear picture of what prospective buyers are likely to challenge during demos, as well as where we need stronger evidence, clearer commercial answers or future product development.

My main takeaway is that we should not treat all ten build candidates as an immediate engineering roadmap. I suggest we separate the work into three areas:

1. **Demo readiness**  
We should verify every “answer now” statement against the current platform and create a concise, rehearsed answer sheet for the three buyer groups: PR agencies, in-house teams and SEO agencies. Some details in the document, including seat rules, action limits, pricing, retention periods, model terminology and security wording, may reflect earlier plans and need checking before we repeat them in a demo.

2. **SEO and sales content**  
The buyer questions give us an excellent next step following the recent SEO work. Two particularly strong opportunities are:
- a transparent methodology resource explaining what we measure, how the process works and its limitations;
- an agency GEO service blueprint showing agencies what they could package and sell to their clients.

These would strengthen demos, answer genuine search demand and improve our credibility without requiring major product development.

3. **Evidence-led product priorities**  
We should use the first round of demos to record which requests are genuine purchase blockers. White-labelled exports, secure sharing, a scores API, coverage import, locale support and wider engine coverage may all be valuable, but I think we should validate the demand before committing them to the roadmap.

There are also four points that need a clear commercial or policy answer rather than a software build:

- ownership and commercial use of generated content;
- the final explanation of team seats;
- a one-page procurement security summary;
- the partner or reseller margin for agencies.

I’ve put together a combined 30, 60 and 90-day action plan covering demo preparation, SEO content, evidence gathering and product prioritisation. The immediate recommendation is to verify the claims, agree the four decisions above and prepare the audience-specific demo scripts before adding new features.

One important point is that I recommend retaining ChatGPT and Claude as the current supported scope unless we make an explicit strategic decision to expand it. We should also remove or properly source any percentages, competitor comparisons and cost claims before using them publicly.

Overall, the question bank gives us a strong framework. The next step is to convert it from a set of hypotheses into an accurate demo and decision-making tool.

Thanks again. It has been very helpful.
