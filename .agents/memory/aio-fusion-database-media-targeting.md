---
name: Database-backed media targeting
description: Product rules separating bespoke business sectors from selectable media promotion categories.
---

Content Creator and Content Optimiser media targets must come from industries represented by accessible, unsuppressed Media Database contacts. Project Set-Up remains free to hold narrower bespoke sectors.

**Why:** Media promotion can only target categories where the workspace can actually reach contacts. Treating bespoke business or audience sectors as selectable media targets creates unsupported targeting claims.

**How to apply:** For fresh content, use section 1.9 only as an exact case-and-whitespace-normalised default against the live database labels. Never union section 1.10, guess semantic mappings, or fall back to static/custom category lists.
