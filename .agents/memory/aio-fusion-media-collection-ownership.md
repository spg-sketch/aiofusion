---
name: Media collection ownership
description: Agreed boundaries for shared media, customer additions, saved references and agency access
---

One centrally maintained AIO Fusion collection is available to signed-in customers. Customer-added contacts and publications are private to the active account; saving any existing record creates a private bookmark to that canonical record, not a new person or publication. A story shortlist is not a reusable saved-media reference, and saving a reference does not claim outreach.

**Why:** The product owners explicitly distinguished a customer's reusable list from both centrally maintained facts and story-specific campaign decisions. Copying shared contacts would create duplicates and let customers accidentally change shared details.

**How to apply:** Apply active-workspace checks to every private media read/write, count, suggestion, linked-journalist list, export and cache. Agency parentage never grants pooled private contact access. Agency staff may see a managed client's private media only while independently authorised and working inside that client's account. Keep historical story decisions and outreach evidence separate from bookmarks.

Customer spreadsheet presentation requests do not authorise replacing the internal full-collection CSV contract or the story-shortlist CSV contract.

**Why:** Personal-list exports serve a different purpose from internal collection maintenance and story outreach. Improving customer-facing spreadsheets must not silently change those separate workflows.

**How to apply:** Consult the canonical customer download policy in replit.md. Preserve Master maintenance and separate story exports, and do not broaden visibility to populate linked-journalist cells.

The shared Search Media Database page may show a dated aggregate collection size, while management totals must still describe only the active workspace's own additions.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the total database size beneath Topic and Authority so customers understand the scale of the shared collection they can search.

**How to apply:** Keep this aggregate informational, not permission to browse or export the full collection. Never include other workspaces' private records in the advertised shared total.
