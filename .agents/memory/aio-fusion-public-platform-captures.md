---
name: AIO Fusion public platform captures
description: Privacy and authenticity boundary for marketing images of authenticated product screens.
---

Marketing screenshots that show the authenticated product should come from a disposable, synthetic workspace running the real app, not from customer accounts or a drawn imitation. Clearly label sample data and empty states.

**Why:** A sign-in-page screenshot and an illustrative UI were originally used where the website brief called for genuine platform views. Capturing real customer workspaces would risk exposing private account and project data.

**How to apply:** Use an isolated local database and synthetic login to capture genuine product views. Inspect exported images for names, emails, URLs and customer content before making them public. Do not call an illustration or sign-in screen an authenticated platform view.

When anonymising an approved marketing capture, replace both the borrowed logo and its adjacent name with a neutral blank mark and sample label. If changing example score graphics for presentation, label the resulting preview illustrative; leave live audit calculations and customer records untouched.

**Why:** The neutral dashboard treatment with distinct example scores was approved, but leaving a brand name below a removed logo would undermine the anonymisation.

**How to apply:** Inspect every logo, caption and score in the final rendered image, not just the source component.
