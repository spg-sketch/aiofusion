---
name: GEO George guide inclusion
description: Published CMS guidance defaults into support search, with an editorial opt-out and no duplicate content.
---

All existing and new published CMS guides should be available in GEO George by default. Editors can exclude individual guides without removing them from the Guidance Library. Drafts must never enter support results.

**Why:** The user chose automatic inclusion with opt-out rather than manually opting each guide in.

**How to apply:** Keep publication status and George eligibility separate. Search authoritative CMS content directly so edits, unpublishing and deletion are reflected in new searches; do not copy guide bodies into the FAQ store.

George's guide support extends the existing retrieval-based support flow, rather than introducing generated answers. Full guides can be read within the support panel.

**Why:** The request was to make existing approved instructions discoverable, not to introduce a new chatbot or replace FAQs.

**How to apply:** Retain FAQ results, distinguish guide sources, provide excerpts and access to full current published instructions, and report search failures separately from genuine no-match results.
