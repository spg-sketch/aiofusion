---
name: AIO Fusion card-free beta trial
description: Durable product rules for beta eligibility, inheritance, allowances, expiry, and conversion.
---

The card-free beta is one authoritative, one-time 60-day period on the top-level billing account. Agency-managed clients inherit their agency's state and cannot start separate trials.

**Why:** No payment method is collected, so expiry cannot auto-charge. Clearing browser data or changing a device clock must not reset or extend access.

**How to apply:** While active, grant 50 paid AI actions per project per 30 days. Direct Client beta accounts receive one project workspace; Agency beta accounts receive two managed-client/one-project packages. Managed clients inherit their agency's beta pool rather than receiving separate trials. After expiry or before activation, keep sign-in and existing data readable but block paid AI actions and new project capacity until checkout activates a subscription. Paid, past-due, master-admin, cancelled, previously used, and permanent free-access accounts cannot start a new trial; free-access remains exempt.

**Client capacity decision:** Enforce the one-project Client beta limit for additional creation without deleting or hiding existing over-limit projects. Keep paid subscription allowances and purchased additional slots separate from this beta restriction.

**Why:** The user explicitly identified multiple projects after Client/Beta onboarding as incorrect. Preserving existing work avoids turning a capacity correction into data loss; it is not permission to create further projects.

**Agency reservation decision:** Empty managed clients reserve capacity. This explicitly supersedes the former shared-project-slot convention in which empty client records were free. Paid Agency capacity includes three managed-client/one-project packages; Direct Clients buy additional projects inside the same account, not child accounts.

**Why:** The confirmed product sells client/project packages, not arbitrary project counts across an Agency subtree. Historical excess must not be deleted to normalize billing.

**How to apply:** Treat legacy Agency-root projects and historical multi-project children conservatively as consumed capacity. Keep Master exceptions explicit; free access does not imply unlimited packages. A reserved client's first project reuses its unit rather than consuming another.
