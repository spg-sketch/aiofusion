---
name: AIO Fusion person vs workspace identity
description: UX rule for distinguishing the authenticated human from the active company or client workspace.
---

Always present the signed-in person and active workspace as two separate identities. Personal names, email addresses, photos and the signed-in state belong to the human; company names, account types and logos belong to the workspace.

**Why:** A workspace slug and logo were previously placed in the personal profile position, making an owner unsure whether they were signed in as themselves or as the company.

**How to apply:** Any account menu, profile page, workspace switcher or impersonation view should answer both questions explicitly: “Who is signed in?” and “Which workspace am I working in?”
